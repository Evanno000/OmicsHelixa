Protein Comparison Screening Criteria

The application currently uses the following minimum thresholds when screening the best alignment for each ORF.

Minimum sequence identity 30%
Minimum query coverage 50%
Minimum reference coverage 20%
Minimum aligned positions 20
Scientific limitation: These are initial screening thresholds for exploratory comparison. They are not universal biological standards. Passing the thresholds does not establish gene function, biological activity, or definitive homology.
